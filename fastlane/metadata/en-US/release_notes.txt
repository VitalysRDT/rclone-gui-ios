What's new in 2.1.2:
- Custom CDN domains: optionally remove a path prefix, such as the bucket name returned by Qiniu Kodo or other S3-compatible storage.
- Cancel PhotoSync: stop the sync, clear its local index and queue, then change the selected albums or destination folder. Files already uploaded remain on the remote.
- PhotoSync albums on Mac: selections update immediately, and only photos from the chosen albums are indexed and synced.
- More reliable PhotoSync pause: it interrupts the current Photos export and rclone batch without pausing your other transfers.
